# Shangrila – character roster

Pre-made characters the spirit can win over (see "Pre-made followers instead of rolled
characters" in [IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md)). They are not placed in towns yet. That comes once the world
map has more places.

- **Tier** is how strong, and how hard to win over, a character is: 1 early and cheap,
  2 mid-game, 3 late and costly.
- **Traits** are hidden from the player and only show through comments and behaviour.
- **Wins them over** is what this character wants (item, gold, faith and power, a task, or
  persuasion).
- Characters marked ★ are tied to the main story.

## Traits

Each character has two or three. A trait shapes comments, combat behaviour and relations.
"Clashes with" is where friction starts; shared or friendly traits build bonds.

| Trait | Shows as | Clashes with |
|---|---|---|
| Brave | Holds the line, rarely breaks | Cowardly |
| Cowardly | May hide or flee when things look bad | Brave, Proud |
| Greedy | Wants a bigger share, may pocket loot | Generous, Honest |
| Generous | Shares, gives food and coin to others | Greedy |
| Pious | Prays, values faith, dislikes blasphemy | Skeptic, Ruthless |
| Skeptic | Doubts the spirit; hard to sway with miracles | Pious, Superstitious |
| Hot-tempered | Attacks instead of parrying, starts quarrels | Calm, Proud |
| Calm | Steady, soothes quarrels | Hot-tempered, Reckless |
| Loyal | Sticks with the party and the host | Fickle, Deceitful |
| Fickle | Mood swings, may leave on a whim | Loyal |
| Proud | Wants respect and to lead, hates retreat | Humble, Cowardly |
| Humble | Content to follow, gets on with most | Proud |
| Curious | Explores, examines, asks questions | Cautious |
| Cautious | Prefers parry and hide, avoids risks | Curious, Reckless |
| Reckless | Charges in, takes chances | Calm, Cautious |
| Ruthless | Kills the fallen, approves of cruelty | Merciful, Pious |
| Merciful | Spares enemies, hates cruelty | Ruthless, Vengeful |
| Vengeful | Never forgets a wrong | Merciful |
| Honest | Speaks the truth, dislikes theft and lies | Deceitful, Greedy |
| Deceitful | Lies easily, may betray | Honest, Loyal |
| Superstitious | Fears omens, the dark, the undead | Skeptic |
| Melancholy | Gloomy, sinks low after losses | Cheerful |
| Cheerful | Lifts spirits around the fire | Melancholy |
| Ambitious | Wants power, may covet the host's role | Humble |
| Bookish | Reads, knows lore, weak at hardship | Reckless |

## The shrine party

The adventurers camping at the roadside shrine on the night the spirit wakes (see "The
awakening"). They are a small mercenary band, the **Harrow Company**, travelling together
because they owe money to the same moneylender in Lehan. The player picks one as the host.

**Joss Harrow** – Human Warrior · Tier 1
Leader of the company, a former town guard thrown out for taking bribes. Wants to pay off
the debt and start over.
Traits: Brave, Greedy, Proud · Wins them over: gold (the debt paid)

**Maera Vell** – Human Ranger · Tier 1
A poacher's daughter who knows every forest path between Lehan and the coast. Joined the
company to get away from home.
Traits: Curious, Loyal, Hot-tempered · Wins them over: a task (find her missing brother)

**Ommuk** – Thuug Cleric · Tier 1
A slow, patient healer who left his marsh village after a bad harvest. Prays to "whoever
listens".
Traits: Pious, Calm, Generous · Wins them over: faith (a miracle he can see)

**Ithrae** – Aeldroe Mage · Tier 1 ★
A young Aeldroe who left her people to study magic. She feels uneasy near the shrine without
knowing why.
Traits: Curious, Melancholy, Honest · Wins them over: persuasion (lore about her people)

**Tikkat** – Nisk Thief · Tier 1
The company's lock-picker, who came cheap and talks too much. He owes the most of all.
Traits: Greedy, Cheerful, Cowardly · Wins them over: gold

## Lehan and its surroundings

**Brother Aldo Quist** – Human Cleric · Tier 1 ★
An initiate of the sect who fled before taking the oath. He knows a few of its signs and
hiding places, and lives in fear of being found.
Traits: Cautious, Pious, Honest · Wins them over: a task (protect him from the sect)

**Hal Venner** – Human Warrior · Tier 1
The mine foreman's son, who went in after the vanished miners and came back alone.
Traits: Brave, Melancholy, Loyal · Wins them over: a task (find out what happened in the
mines)

**Lisbet Farrow** – Human Thief · Tier 1
Runs errands, and rumours, for the inn. Knows everyone in Lehan and sells what she knows.
Traits: Deceitful, Cheerful, Curious · Wins them over: gold

**Boddo** – Thuug Warrior · Tier 1
A dock porter who can carry a cart on his back. Slow to anger and slower to forget.
Traits: Calm, Vengeful, Humble · Wins them over: a task (a debt of honour)

**Skee** – Nisk Ranger · Tier 1
A rat-catcher in Lehan's cellars who has seen strange lights in the old tunnels below the
town.
Traits: Superstitious, Curious, Cautious · Wins them over: persuasion

**Tamsin Rook** – Human Enchanter · Tier 2
A charming fortune-teller at the market whose fortunes come true a little too often.
Traits: Ambitious, Deceitful, Curious · Wins them over: faith and power

**Garreth Holm** – Human Paladin · Tier 2 ★
A knight of an old Lehan family, one of the families the sect hides among. He does not know
his family's oath.
Traits: Honest, Proud, Pious · Wins them over: persuasion (shared values)

## The roads and wilds

**Wenna Strand** – Human Ranger · Tier 1
A messenger who rides between towns and hears every rumour on the road.
Traits: Cheerful, Reckless, Fickle · Wins them over: gold

**Huurm** – Thuug Monk · Tier 2
A hermit who has sat by the same river for forty years and remembers roads no map shows.
Traits: Calm, Humble, Bookish · Wins them over: a task (bring him a lost book)

**Mivvi** – Nisk Enchanter · Tier 1
A travelling tinker who sells charms that mostly work.
Traits: Cheerful, Greedy, Superstitious · Wins them over: an item (a rare trinket)

**Vardan the Renegade** – Human Warrior · Tier 2
A deserter with a price on his head (the surviving painting "The Enemy"). Hunted, bitter
and dangerous, but he keeps his word.
Traits: Ruthless, Loyal, Hot-tempered · Wins them over: a task (clear his name)

**Taggu** – Thuug Ranger · Tier 1
A ferry-keeper who walks across the river bottom when the ferry breaks.
Traits: Humble, Generous, Cautious · Wins them over: gold

**Rennik** – Nisk Warrior · Tier 2
A sell-sword who fights with two short blades and trusts no one without whiskers.
Traits: Vengeful, Brave, Skeptic · Wins them over: gold and a share of loot

**Piet Dunmore** – Human Mage · Tier 2
A disgraced scholar thrown out of his academy for digging into forbidden histories, among
them the fall of Shangrila.
Traits: Bookish, Proud, Curious · Wins them over: an item (an old text about Shangrila)

## Thuug marshes

**Orrun** – Thuug Paladin · Tier 2
A shield-bearer of the marsh villages, sworn to protect travellers on the causeways.
Traits: Loyal, Brave, Honest · Wins them over: persuasion (a good deed he witnesses)

**Ubba** – Thuug Cleric · Tier 2
A village elder's wife who remembers stories her grandmother told about a cruel ruler from
the hills.
Traits: Generous, Superstitious, Merciful · Wins them over: faith

**Grath-Ul** – Thuug Warrior · Tier 3
The strongest wrestler in the marshes, unbeaten for twenty years and bored of it.
Traits: Proud, Reckless, Cheerful · Wins them over: a task (beat him in a contest)

**Kesh-Daal** – Thuug Mage · Tier 3
An ancient Thuug who remembers some of the old roads and the old rulers, and keeps her own
counsel.
Traits: Bookish, Cautious, Skeptic · Wins them over: faith and power

## Aeldroe lands

**Veylin** – Aeldroe Ranger · Tier 2
A scout who guards the borders of the Aeldroe valleys and distrusts all outsiders.
Traits: Loyal, Cautious, Vengeful · Wins them over: persuasion (proof of good intent)

**Miraen** – Aeldroe Enchanter · Tier 2 ★
Granddaughter of an Aeldroe elder. She has overheard part of "the secret" and is
determined to learn the rest.
Traits: Curious, Ambitious, Honest · Wins them over: a task (help her learn the secret)

**Thaelis** – Aeldroe Monk · Tier 2
A healer who tends Aeldroes so faded they can barely be seen. Gentle, and quietly furious.
Traits: Merciful, Melancholy, Calm · Wins them over: persuasion (a promise to help her
people)

**Caelin** – Aeldroe Thief · Tier 1
A young Aeldroe who uses his near-invisibility to steal from humans, as a kind of revenge.
Traits: Reckless, Vengeful, Cheerful · Wins them over: gold

**Liraeth** – Aeldroe Cleric · Tier 3 ★
One of the elders who keep "the secret". She would never knowingly serve the spirit, but
could be won over by someone she does not recognise as it. Touching her is dangerous: she
would recognise the spirit at once.
Traits: Pious, Proud, Merciful · Wins them over: faith and power, or deceit

**Nimae** – Aeldroe Mage · Tier 3
A powerful sorceress who believes the Aeldroes' fading can be reversed by force, and does
not care how.
Traits: Ambitious, Ruthless, Bookish · Wins them over: an item (a remnant-touched relic)

## Nisk warrens

**Sasska** – Nisk Thief · Tier 2
A burrow-boss who runs a ring of smugglers in the tunnels under several towns.
Traits: Greedy, Deceitful, Ambitious · Wins them over: gold, a lot of it

**Pip-Tooth** – Nisk Monk · Tier 1
A tiny, wiry fighter who trained himself by watching others and copying them.
Traits: Cheerful, Brave, Humble · Wins them over: persuasion

**Kesk** – Nisk Ranger · Tier 2
A tunnel guide who knows ways under the mountains that no surface map shows.
Traits: Cautious, Loyal, Superstitious · Wins them over: a task (save his warren from a
flood)

**Grisk** – Nisk Cleric · Tier 2
A healer of the warrens who mixes medicine and poison with equal skill.
Traits: Skeptic, Calm, Greedy · Wins them over: an item (a rare herb)

## Further afield (late game)

**Ysolde Marrin** – Human Paladin · Tier 3
A famous champion, retired after a war she does not talk about.
Traits: Melancholy, Honest, Brave · Wins them over: a task (one last cause worth fighting
for)

**Edrin Calloway** – Human Enchanter · Tier 3 ★
A wandering minstrel with a strange, inherited melody. He is a descendant of the beloved's
lover, though he does not know it. The melody was the lover's. Meeting him is a sharp test of
redemption or relapse.
Traits: Cheerful, Fickle, Merciful · Wins them over: persuasion

**Brannoc Hale** – Human Warrior · Tier 3
A warlord's former captain who would serve whoever pays best, and wins.
Traits: Ruthless, Ambitious, Greedy · Wins them over: gold and power

**Odo Brakk** – Human Monk · Tier 2
A former soldier of the plague years who walks from town to town burying the dead.
Traits: Humble, Melancholy, Pious · Wins them over: faith

**Aldric Thorne** – Human Mage · Tier 3 ★
A respected scholar who seems eager to help. He is secretly a sect agent sent to get
close to the spirit, and may betray the party at a key moment.
Traits: Deceitful, Bookish, Calm (seems Loyal) · Wins them over: offers himself

**Saelith** – Aeldroe Warrior · Tier 2
Ithrae's brother. Left home to bring her back, and does not approve of the company she keeps.
Traits: Proud, Loyal, Hot-tempered · Wins them over: a task (it depends on what happened to
Ithrae)

**Ollo Chitterling** – Nisk Mage · Tier 3 ★
A warren seer who has dreamed of a crowned figure under the mountain since childhood. The
other Nisk think she is mad.
Traits: Superstitious, Melancholy, Curious · Wins them over: faith (a dream sent by the
spirit, R4)
