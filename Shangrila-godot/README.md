# Shangrila (Godot recreation)

A Godot 4 recreation of *Shangrila*, an unfinished Macintosh adventure game written in
Pascal in 1997 (the original source, mainly `Shangrila.p` and `ShangrilaUtils.p`, is not included in this repository).

Run it: open the folder in Godot 4.7 and press Play, or run `godot-4 --path .` from this folder.

## What was carried over from the original

- **Screens and flow:** About picture, then main menu (New / Resume / Quit), Inn, View
  Character, Weapon Smith and the Mines. The original's Create Character screen and painted
  city view are retired: characters are pre-made, and Lehan is now a town map (see below).
- **Character rules:** 3×(2–6) stat rolls, hit points, endurance, the starting
  purse, class requirements, the 22 skills and their formulas, starting kits per class, and max
  encumbrance. The bonus tables are copied exactly from `ShangrilaUtils.p`.
- **Items:** the full item pool (31 items) with the original weights, defense values, slot rules
  (`DoesItFit`), food effects (`EatAndDrink`), armour class and to-hit calculations.
- **Inn:** a roster of 23 characters, a party of 6, plus Muster / Dismiss / Info / Kill / scroll.
- **View Character:** drag and drop between the body slots and the 18-slot backpack. Drop an item
  on Info to examine it, on Eat to eat it, on Trash to throw it away, or on another character's
  portrait to give it to them.
- **Shop:** the Lehan weapon smith stock (including the three duplicate longswords), plus the
  basket and money display (1 G = 10 S = 100 C).
- **Mines:** the exact 66-square map from `InitData`, with movement (turn, step, strafe), the
  compass and map mode with a pulsating party marker. The view is now real 3D instead of the
  original's pre-drawn strips.
- **Art:** the original PICT files in `Other Stuff` were decoded to PNG: the portraits
  "The Man" and "The Enemy", the sword, sky, rock textures and coins. The stone wall,
  floor tiles, parchment and chain textures are generated by `tools/make_textures.py`.

## What had to be filled in

- **Races:** the original Dwarf, Elf, Half Elf, Halfling and Human are replaced by Humans
  (+1 Str), Thuugs (slow, turtle-like folk: +1 Str, +2 Con, -2 Dex, -1 Cha, +1 armour class
  from the shell), Aeldroes (slim, half-transparent beings with a gift for magic: +2 Int, +1 Will,
  -1 Str, -2 Con, +10 Hide and Magic Arts) and Nisk (wiry rat-folk: +2 Dex, +1 Con, -1 Str,
  -1 Cha, +10 Climb, +5 Perception). Old saves are converted when loaded.
- The resource forks (all UI pictures, the item icons and the 47 portraits) were empty in the
  copy, so the UI is rebuilt in code. Item icons and most portraits are drawn procedurally.
- Save and Resume were stubs in the original. Here they save to `user://shangrila_save.json`.
- Pay, Talk and Haggle in the shop had buttons but no logic. Pay now buys the basket, while Talk
  and Haggle only produce flavour text.
- Combat was never written (only the nine buttons existed), so it is new. See below.
  Examine only prints a message. Sleep now heals: it turns endurance (the apple bar) into
  hit points and restores mana, but monsters may interrupt.
- Six passages in the map lead to squares that were never defined (the map was unfinished).
  They show as blocked by rubble.

## Prologue (new)

*New* on the main menu starts with the opening scene from `DIALOGUES.md` in the repository root:
the spirit's eyes open on the Harrow Company's camp at dawn, it listens to their morning chatter
(its own thoughts in italics), chooses whom to hold on to, and makes the first choice through
that host in the new dialogue screen (keys 1-9). The scene moves on by itself; click or Space
hurries it, P pauses, and the mouse wheel (or H) opens the history of everything said so far. The screen shows the speaker, the host's inner voice in italics, a "Speaking" row
(only the host can speak for now) and replies tagged with the host's traits. The text is in
`scripts/data/prologue.gd`, the portraits for the four races in `scripts/widgets/race_portrait.gd`.

After the prologue the five members of the Harrow Company become the party, with the host first.
Their stats are rolled to fit their race and class, and their hidden traits are stored on them.
The party starts at the ruined shrine on the world map, a day from Lehan. The mines are not on
the map yet: the crier's notice in Lehan's market square puts them there.

## The road to Lehan and the first fight (new)

After the prologue the world map opens and the company sets out at once. The red marker moves
along the road while a dialogue window over the map plays the chatter (the companions begin to
notice something odd about the host, and the player chooses the host's reply). Then brigands
ambush the company: the first fight, in a battle window over the map. Everyone fights on their
own, by their tactics and traits (a Cowardly character hides, a Cautious one may parry). Before
each round the spirit can only help its host: *Guide the blow* (better aim and a little more
damage) or *Ward* (the next blow aimed at the host turns aside). Afterwards the companions
react to what they saw, the spirit takes full hold of its host, and the company reaches Lehan.
The text is in `scripts/data/road.gd`, the window in `scripts/widgets/battle_window.gd`, and the
dialogue window used by both scenes in `scripts/widgets/dialogue_box.gd`.

## Town map (new)

Lehan is a town plan in the style of the world map (generated by `tools/make_textures.py`).
It shows the places found so far: at first the South Gate, the Market Square and the Inn. The
market's crier puts the Mines on the world map and points out the Weapon Smith. Places are
listed in `TOWNS` in `scripts/game.gd`.

## World map (new)

The town gate opens a map of the known lands (generated by `tools/make_textures.py`). It shows
the places the party has discovered, with dashed routes between them, and a red ring where the
party is. Click the party's own place (or *Enter*) to go in, or another place to travel there (the red
marker moves along the road).
Each day on the road costs every character 8 Endurance (the apple bar), so bring food. There are
three places so far: the Old Shrine, Lehan and the Mines, each a day apart along the road. Places and routes are listed in
`LOCATIONS` and `ROUTES` in `scripts/game.gd`.

## Combat (new)

Monsters can appear as you walk (12% per step after a few quiet steps). The deeper you go, the
tougher they get: rats, bats and kobolds near the entrance, then goblins, spiders and skeletons,
and finally orcs and renegades (the surviving "The Enemy" painting).

- **Turn based:** each round you give every living character an order (Attack, Parry, Spells,
  Use, Equip, Turn, Flee, Hide, Pass), clicking the monster group or portrait to target. Then
  everyone acts in initiative order and the log plays out at a readable pace. Click the log or
  press Space to hurry. Esc takes back the previous order.
- **Default actions:** *Tactics* sets a default action per character. *Defaults* gives everyone
  who still has no order their default and fights the round.
- **Auto-combat** (off at the start of every fight): toggle *Auto* and every round uses the
  default actions and races through. Untoggle it to take over again.
- Rules: hit chance uses the original weapon skills and armour class. Weapons now have damage
  ranges. The first three party members take most of the blows. Parry makes you harder to hit.
  Hide makes monsters ignore you and doubles your next hit. Clerics and paladins can turn undead.
  Bows need arrows somewhere on the character (they are picked up again after the fight).
- Spells: mages (Fire Dart), enchanters (Slumber) and clerics (Mend) now get a mana pool from
  Magic Arts (the original kept max magic at 0).
- Winning gives experience, coins and sometimes an item. Fallen characters come around with 1 hit
  point. If the whole party falls, they wake up at the inn, robbed of half their coins.
  (Experience has no use yet: the original had no levels.)

## Controls in the mines

W/↑ forward, S/↓ back, A/← and D/→ turn, Q/E step sideways, M map.
In combat: A Attack, P Parry, S Spells, U Use, E Equip, T Turn, F Flee, H Hide, X Pass,
D Defaults, Esc back, Space hurry.

## Developer aid

`godot-4 --path . -- --tour=$PWD/.tour` visits every screen, saves screenshots into `.tour/`,
and runs a few rule checks (see `scripts/dev_tour.gd`). It also simulates 200 auto-fights
at several depths and prints the win rate, which is handy when tuning monsters in `scripts/combat.gd`.
